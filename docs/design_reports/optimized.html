05 / Scope & traceability

Model notes & engineering data

Keep assumptions and validity limits attached to the predictions.

Obstacle and structural interpretation

Requested endpoint rock pose is geometrically reachable; this alone does not establish traversal.

Obstacle drum–wheel clearance

Requested-height obstacle clearance screen. Parallel finite cylinders: shared roll is removed, then circular-plane and axial-width gaps are combined. Negative gap means overlap of the ideal solids. A common roll rotation preserves relative clearance in both models; rocker articulation still changes relative geometry.

Ditch requirement remains unverified. Static slope tipping assumes adequate friction; it does not verify climbing. The 5 mm structural limit applies to the modeled envelope of local deflections, not one assembled-frame displacement.

For the plate frame, aluminum cross-tube stiffness/strength are PROVISIONAL. Connections, dynamic obstacle loads, cutting/recovery frame cases and whole-plate buckling need separate verification.

Standard X44 motors · assumptions

Four wheel motors at 100:1; two drum motors at 40.30:1 (exploratory gearing).

Electrical energy = output-shaft mechanical work / combined efficiency. Drive: 72%; drums: 70%. Both efficiencies are provisional.

Continuous operation unverified. Current-limit inputs of zero mean undecided. Shaft torque-speed capability is enforced separately from energy accounting.

PA-04 actuators · 900 lbf / 12 V

Two actuators. Continuous catalog stroke: 162.5 mm; installed hardware limits: 523.7 mm to 686.2 mm. These include 161.3 mm added to each endpoint. Compact, travel, deposition and digging commands are compared with these limits diagnostically; packaging is not an active constraint.

Mass per actuator: 2.44 kg. Raising envelope: 0.00991 m/s and 14.0 A; raising time: 9.97 s, lowering time: 9.97 s. Lowering assumes rated-load speed and 14 A. Electrical energy uses 12 V × current × time for both actuators, including motor losses.

Mass fit supports 2–40 inch strokes; load curves support 0–900 lbf. Guarded out-of-domain diagnostics retain failed checks. Independent hardware, gravity-assisted lowering, duty cycle, temperature and installation-hardware mass remain unverified. A continuous stroke must be replaced with an available catalog size and reevaluated before purchase.
